Unit 4: Transport & Directions

Navigate cities confidently with these essential travel phrases.

💡 Lola's Travel Tip: Getting Around

Apps like Yandex Go work perfectly in major Uzbek cities and show the exact price upfront. If you stop a taxi on the street, always agree on the price before getting in!

Uzbek Pronunciation Meaning Usage & Context
Qayerda? khah-yer-DAH? Where is...? "Metro qayerda?" (Where is the metro?)
Chapga chahp-GAH To the left Directing a taxi driver.
O‘ngga oong-GAH To the right Hand gestures also help!
To‘g‘riga toogh-ree-GAH Straight ahead Keep going straight.
Shu yerda to‘xtang shoo yer-DAH tookh-TAHNG Stop right here Very useful when you arrive.
Aeroport / Vokzal ah-eh-roh-PORT / vohk-ZAHL Airport / Station State your destination clearly.
Chipta cheep-TAH Ticket Train, museum, or bus ticket.
Taksi chaqiring tahk-SEE chah-khi-REENG Call a taxi Ask hotel reception with this phrase.
Manzil mahn-ZEEL Address Show your written address.
Qancha vaqt? KHAHN-chah vahkht? How long? Asking about travel duration.
💬 In the Taxi

You: Vokzal qayerda?

Local: To'g'riga, keyin chapga.

You: Taksi qancha vaqt ketadi?

Local: O'n minut. (10 minutes)

🚆 Fast Trains

Uzbekistan’s high-speed train, Afrosiyob, is the best way to travel between Tashkent, Samarkand, and Bukhara. Book tickets weeks in advance!
