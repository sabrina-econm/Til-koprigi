Unit 3: Numbers & Money

Essential numbers for paying taxis, counting items, and shopping.

💵 Local Currency: So'm (UZS)

Cash is essential for bazaars, local taxis, and small shops. The national currency is the So'm (pronounced sohm). It's very common to deal in thousands, so learning "Yuz" (100) and "Ming" (1,000) is key!

💬 Taxi Practice

Driver: Yigirma ming. (20,000)

You: Mana, oling. (Here, take it)

Driver: Qaytimingiz. (Your change)

You: Rahmat!

✨ Handing Money

It is considered polite to hand money or receive change using your right hand, or with both hands for extra respect.
