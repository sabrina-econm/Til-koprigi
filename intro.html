4 Unique Uzbek Sounds to Master

O‘ o‘ Deep rounded "aw" (like law) • Ex: O‘zbekiston
G‘ g‘ Guttural throat "R" (like French Paris) • Ex: G‘alaba
Q q Deep uvular "K" sound • Ex: Qayerda? (Where is it?)
X / H X: Raspy throat friction (Xayr) | H: Soft breath (Hozir)
