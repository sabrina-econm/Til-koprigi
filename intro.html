🔤 4 Unique Uzbek Sounds to Master

Uzbek uses the modern Latin script. Familiarize yourself with these 4 sounds:

O‘ o‘
Deep rounded "aw" (like saw or law)
Example: O‘zbekiston (Uzbekistan)
G‘ g‘
Soft guttural friction (like French or German throat R)
Example: G‘alaba (Victory)
Q q
Emphatic back-of-the-throat "K" sound
Example: Qayerda? (Where is it?)
X / H
X: Raspy throat friction (Scottish loch)
H: Soft gentle breath (English hotel)
Example: Xayr (Goodbye) vs Hozir (Now)
