Unit 5: Food & Choyxona

Savor authentic Uzbek flavors and master traditional dining etiquette.

💡 Lola's Tip: Bread & Tea Etiquette

Never place bread (Non) upside down! Always tear it with your hands. When pouring tea, pour it into your bowl and back into the teapot 3 times (qaytarish) to properly brew it.

Uzbek Pronunciation Meaning Usage & Context
Mazali! mah-zah-LEE Delicious! Great compliment to the chef.
Osh / Palov ohsh / pah-LOHV Uzbek Pilaf The national dish. A must-try!
Choy choy Tea Specify "Ko'k" (Green) or "Qora" (Black).
Go‘sht goosht Meat Usually beef or lamb.
Suv soov Water "Gazsiz suv" means still water.
Qornim to‘q qohr-NEEM tookq I am full Polite way to refuse more food.
Hisob, iltimos hee-SOHP eel-ti-MOHS The bill, please Ask the waiter when ready to pay.
Achchiq emasmi? ahch-CHIHQ eh-MAHS-mee Is it spicy? Useful if you prefer mild food.
Go‘shtsiz bormi? goosht-SEEZ bohr-MEE Anything meatless? Helpful phrase for vegetarians.
Somsangiz bormi? sohm-sahn-GEEZ bohr-mee Have Somsa? Somsa is a baked savory pastry.
💬 Ordering Food

You: Menu bering, iltimos.

Waiter: Marhamat. Nima yeysiz?

You: Bitta Osh va ko'k choy.

Waiter: Hozir keladi!

🪑 The Tapchan

In traditional Choyxonas (teahouses), you may sit cross-legged on a raised wooden platform called a Tapchan. Always take your shoes off before stepping on it!
