Unit 1: Essential Greetings & Courtesies

Master authentic Uzbek greetings and respectful everyday expressions.

💡 Lola's Cultural Etiquette: The Heart Gesture

Place your right hand gently over your heart and bow slightly. It communicates profound warmth, respect, and humility without needing physical contact.

Uzbek Pronunciation Meaning Lola's Etiquette Tip
Assalomu alaykum ah-sah-LOH-moo ah-LYE-koom Peace be upon you Right hand on heart, slight bow.
Va alaykum assalom vah ah-LYE-koom ah-SAH-lohm And unto you peace Reply with a warm, genuine smile.
Salom sah-LOHM Hi / Hello Informal; avoid using with elders.
Rahmat rahkh-MAHT Thank you Say Katta rahmat! for deep thanks.
Iltimos eel-tee-MOHS Please Instantly softens any request.
Kechirasiz keh-chee-rah-SEEZ Excuse me / Sorry Use when calling waitstaff politely.
Xush kelibsiz! khoosh keh-LEEP-seez Welcome! Respond with a warm Rahmat!
Yaxshimisiz? yakh-shee-mee-SEEZ? How are you? Standard reply: Yaxshi, rahmat!
Xayr KHYE-ur Goodbye Accompany with a polite nod or wave.
Maylimi? mye-lee-MEE? May I? / Okay? Ask before taking photos of elders.
💬 Mini Practice

Host: Assalomu alaykum! Xush kelibsiz!

You: Va alaykum assalom! Rahmat!

Host: Yaxshimisiz? — You: Yaxshi, rahmat!

✨ Cultural Note

Asking about health and family well-being before discussing any business or questions is a cornerstone of Uzbek hospitality and courtesy.
