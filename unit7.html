Unit 7: Emergencies & Help

Essential phrases for staying safe, finding a pharmacy, or asking for help.

🚨 Lola's Safety Note

Uzbekistan is very safe, but be prepared. Dial 102 for Police, and 103 for an Ambulance. Look for "Dorixona" signs for a pharmacy.

Uzbek Pronunciation Meaning Usage & Context
Yordam bering! yor-DAHM beh-REENG Help me! Use this to get immediate attention.
Kasalman kah-SAHL-mahn I am sick Tell this to your hotel receptionist.
Dorixona qayerda? do-ri-kho-NAH kha-yer-DAH Pharmacy? Many pharmacies work 24/7.
Shifokor kerak shi-fo-KOHR keh-RAHK Need a doctor If you need medical professional help.
Tez yordam tez yor-DAHM Ambulance Literally means "fast help".
Og‘riyapti ogh-ree-YAHP-ti It hurts Point to the area and say this.
Yo‘qolib qoldim yo-kho-LEEP qol-DEEM I am lost Locals will happily guide you back.
Telefoningizni bering te-le-fon-in-GIZ-ni be-REENG Use your phone? To call your guide or emergency.
Politsiya po-LEET-si-ya Police Tourist police wear bright vests.
Elchixona el-chi-kho-NAH Embassy Ask taxi drivers to take you there.
💬 At the Pharmacy

You: Salom, men kasalman.

Pharmacist: Nima og'riyapti? (What hurts?)

You: Boshim og'riyapti. (My head hurts)

👮 Tourist Police

In major tourist cities like Samarkand and Bukhara, you will see Tourist Police. They often speak English and are specifically there to help you!
